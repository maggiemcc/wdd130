Splash: White Water Rafting

Here at Splash, our mission is to provide thrill-seekers with an unforgettable and safe experience. Our expert guides ensure everyone is safe and has fun while on the water so they can enjoy the beautiful views and the wild rapids.

History

Splash was founded in 1994 by Sam Perkins. He grew up as an outdoor adventure enthusiast who wanted others to be able to experience the beautiful outdoors too.

He started with only a handful of rafts and slowly grew the company to be what it is today. A family-friendly, professional, and safe water rafting company that gives all an opportunity to enjoy the great outdoors.
